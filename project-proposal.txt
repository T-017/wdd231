Week 4 Project Proposal

Project Subject
- Traveling to and learning about Japan

Project Description
The site will provide information such as:
- Site links to travel agencies
- Popular tourist destinations providing map locations (with real time weather updates)
- Recomeended hotel accomodations
- Transportation methods with instructions
- Information request form and subscription to site news and updates
- A thank you page for submitting a request form
- A brief introduction to cultural norms in Japan

Project Reason and Purpose
- I am half Japanese and would like to learn more about my culture while also preparing to visit, as my wife if Japanese and would like to take me there some day
- My wife also points out the common issues that Japanese people have with tourist and their cultural insensitivity
- This subject is both interesting and relavent to me, hence the reason why I chose it as the subject
- To educate travelers about the culture of Japan that goes beyond main stream pop culture 
- Also to provide tourist with general travel information
