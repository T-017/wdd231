Non-Profit Membership Details

  • Free membership for non-profit organizations.
  • Listing in member directory.

  • All Bronze benefits.
  • Enhanced promotional opportunities.
  • Priority listing in member directory.

Gold Membership Details

  • Annual fee: $200
  • All Silver benefits.
  • Premium exposure on chamber website and events.
  • Sponsorship opportunities for chamber events.
